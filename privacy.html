Privacy Policy

Tellu: Questions for Couples · last updated 6 October 2026

The short version

Tellu is a deck of conversation cards for two people sharing one phone. Everything you type into it — your names, the notes you keep about what you learned, the cards you write yourselves — stays on your iPhone. We have no accounts, no servers of our own and no way to read your notebook.

What stays on your device

Player names, the warmth you pick for the evening, which cards you have already played, your evenings and notes, and your own cards are stored locally in the app's private storage. Deleting the app, or using “Erase history” in Settings, removes them. They are not sent to us or to anyone else and are not used for advertising.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card details or Apple ID. To check whether your subscription is active we use Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc.), which receives an anonymous app installation identifier, the products you bought, purchase and renewal dates, your App Store country and basic device information (model, iOS version). Adapty processes this data on our behalf only to manage subscriptions and to show us aggregated sales numbers. See adapty.io/privacy.

What we do not do

No sign-in, no contacts, no location, no camera or microphone, no analytics SDKs beyond subscription status, no ad tracking, no data sold or shared with data brokers.

Children

Tellu is made for adult couples and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights and contact

Because your content never leaves your device, you control it fully. For questions about subscription data processed by Adapty, or anything in this policy, write to netkaktakto@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
