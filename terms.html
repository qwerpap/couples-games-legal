Tellu: Questions for Couples · last updated 6 October 2026

The app

Tellu gives you decks of questions, small dares and “this or that” prompts to play together on one phone. Two decks are free forever. The full table — all decks, your own cards and new decks we add — needs an active subscription.

Subscriptions

Full table is offered as a weekly or yearly auto-renewing subscription; prices are shown in the app before you buy. A free trial, when offered, converts to a paid subscription at the end of the trial unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase (or when the trial ends). The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Playing kindly

The cards are prompts, not instructions. Skip anything that doesn't feel right; nobody has to answer. Tellu is not therapy or counselling and is not a substitute for professional help.

Your cards and our cards

The cards you write belong to you and stay on your device. The decks that ship with Tellu are our original writing; please don't copy them into other products.
