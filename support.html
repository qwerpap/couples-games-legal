Tellu: Questions for Couples

Write to us

Email netkaktakto@gmail.com, or use Settings › Write to us inside the app — it adds your app version so we can help faster. We answer within two working days.

Common questions

I paid but decks are still locked. Open the Pro screen or Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel? iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Tellu.

Where are my notes? On this iPhone only, in the Notebook. They are not synced and are erased when the app is deleted.

A card felt off. Skip it — and tell us which one; we rewrite cards every month.
